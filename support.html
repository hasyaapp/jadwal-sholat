Jadwal Sholat & Imsakiyah support

Found a time that differs from your local schedule, or need help? Email asharyhsm@gmail.com. Please include the city, the date, and the time you expected.

Questions

  • Why can the start of Ramadan differ? The app estimates 1 Ramadan from the arithmetic Hijri calendar. Indonesia fixes it by sidang isbat, so adjust the date in the app to the official decision.
  • Why are Terbit and Dhuha missing? Canva tables hold at most 225 cells. A full month fits the five daily prayers; the Ramadan table adds Imsak.
  • Which time zone is used? WIB, WITA or WIT, following the province, and shown in the table title.
