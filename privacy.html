Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This policy covers the Jadwal Sholat & Imsakiyah app for Canva ("the app"), published by Aksara Nusantara.

What we collect

Nothing. Prayer times are calculated inside your browser from a city list built into the app. Your choices are not sent to us or to any third party, and the app has no server, no accounts, no analytics, and no cookies of its own.

Canva

The app runs inside Canva. Tables you add to a design are stored by Canva as part of that design, under Canva's privacy policy.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this changes, we will update this page before the new version of the app is released. Questions: asharyhsm@gmail.com.
