Jadwal Sholat & Imsakiyah

A Canva app that adds an editable prayer-time table for any of 517 Indonesian cities and regencies: a monthly schedule of the five daily prayers, or a Ramadan imsakiyah with Imsak.

Times follow the Kemenag RI criteria (Subuh −20°, Isya −18°, Ashar shadow 1×, ihtiyath) and match the published Kemenag schedule to within one minute.
