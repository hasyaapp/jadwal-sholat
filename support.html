Jadwal Sholat & Imsakiyah support

Found a time that differs from your local schedule, or need help? Email asharyhsm@gmail.com. Please include the city, the date, and the time you expected.

Questions

  • Why can Hijri dates or the start of Ramadan differ? The app uses the arithmetic Hijri calendar. Indonesia fixes month starts by rukyat and sidang isbat, so shift the Hijri dates (−2 to +2 days) or the first day of Ramadan in the app to match the official decision.
  • Why are Terbit and Dhuha missing? Canva tables hold at most 225 cells. A full month fits the date, the Hijri date and the five daily prayers; the Ramadan table adds Imsak.
  • Where are the title and source note? They are added as text above and below the table (or beside it on wide pages), so you can move, restyle or delete them.
  • Can I change how dates look? Yes: pick a date format (01 Kamis, Kamis, 1, Kam, 01 or Kamis, 1 Okt), turn the Hijri column on or off, and highlight Subuh and Maghrib in Ramadan tables.
  • Which time zone is used? WIB, WITA or WIT, following the province, and shown in the title.
